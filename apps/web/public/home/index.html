How it works

Live in three steps

1

Print your QR cards

Every table gets its own QR. Generate and print them from your dashboard in minutes — no hardware, no POS terminal.

2

Diners scan & order

The QR opens your live menu in their browser — no app download, no sign-up. They customise dishes and order from the seat.

3

Kitchen sees it instantly

Orders land on your live board in real time with a chime. Accept → prepare → serve, while diners track every step.

Payments

Diners pay through a UPI QR that points at your UPI ID, cash at the counter, or card checkout on your own gateway account. Menutha never touches the money and never takes a cut — our only price is a flat monthly subscription.
