Menutha Partner — corrected build target

The Claude Design pack, rendered standalone, with the locked product decisions applied. This is the file the app is being built against.
Corrections applied to the pack — the locked rejects:
1 · “Ready now” removed from the Orders tickets — they carry Edit + Cancel only. It marked a status the board does not act on, and it carried the only path that pushed a diner about their food.
2 · Print paper-size picker removed from Billing. The pack offered 80 mm / 58 mm / A4 chips above the Print bill button; paper is chosen in the system print dialog and the sheet adapts to it. The “prints a TAX INVOICE…” note stays, since it still describes what comes out.
3 · ACCEPT actions removed from Notifications — three of them, across the lock-screen card, the expanded card and the in-app banner. Orders are live the moment a diner confirms; there is nothing to accept. The banner reading “Order #1043 accepted” now reads “placed”, which is what actually happened. Settings’ Accept parcel orders toggle is an unrelated setting and is untouched.
4 · Sign-up payment step deferred — the pack’s step 6 was a Razorpay UPI-AutoPay / card / net-banking mandate. Razorpay is not wired yet, so that step is gone and sign-up is 5 steps, not 6 (every “Step N of 6” relabelled). It now ends at plan selection, with a Skip — decide later option beside it. Three lines of copy had to follow, because they promised things that no longer happen: the plan CTA was “Continue · ₹999/mo after trial” → “Start the 10-day trial”; “your card is authorised for AutoPay after day 10” → no card is taken at all; and the success screen’s “renews ₹999/mo unless you cancel” → nothing renews, because no mandate exists.
5 · Plan periods completed — Monthly ₹999 · 6 months ₹5,399 · Annual ₹9,599. The pack already had ₹999 and ₹9,599; the six-month row was missing.
Left as the pack has them (flagged, not changed): Basic ₹599 and Enterprise ₹3,499 are tier prices on a different axis from the Growth periods above; the pack’s own handoff calls them placeholders pending the subscription_plans table. Confirm before they reach a customer.
Also left: the Plan screen still carries the footnote “payments are processed by Razorpay”. That is a post-sign-up screen and Razorpay is queued for it, so it was outside the sign-up correction — but it is a promise the app cannot keep today. Say the word and it comes out too.
The two grey cells are Apple’s and Google’s own sheets — shown so the flow reads end to end, but they are drawn by the OS, not by us. Nothing in them is ours to build or restyle.
Where the pack’s HANDOFF.md disagrees with its screens, the screens were taken as the source and corrected directly — the prose is stale in every place it conflicts.
