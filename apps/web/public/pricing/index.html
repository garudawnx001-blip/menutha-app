Questions, answered

Do you really take no commission?

Really. Diner payments go straight to your UPI ID, your counter, or your own gateway account. Menutha's revenue is the subscription — that's the whole model.

How do I pay for the subscription?

UPI Autopay or card e-mandate through Razorpay — set up once from your dashboard, cancel anytime. If a charge fails you get a 7-day grace period before ordering pauses; your menu never disappears.

What happens after the 10-day trial?

Pick a plan to keep going. If you don't, your public menu stays visible but table ordering pauses until you subscribe.

Do my diners need to install anything?

No. The QR opens your menu in their phone's browser — order and pay with nothing to download.

Can I change or cancel plans?

Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from the dashboard at any time. Cancellations apply at the end of the billing cycle.
